Why do neural networks generalize?

Despite the amazing success ChatGPT AlphaFold , etc. their exist no good reason why minimizing the loss on a training set would allow a model to make reliable predictions on some test data, which is out of sample.

That is when you minimize the loss why don’t you simply memorize the training data ? Where does generalization come from?

Here I argue it is a fundamental property of renormalization group theory and the scale invariant correlation structure of natural data

Every layer in a normal network is a quenched view of the correlation structure of the data and as the neural network converges the individual layers, capture the scale invariant correlations in the training data sample(s)

Then when you try to make a prediction on a test sample, if the test sample has the same scale in variant correlation structure that is, it looks reasonably similar to the training data under this view the network can make a reliable prediction

This gives deep insight into what it means to be in distribution versus out of distribution

Going well beyond statistics, this is a fundamental property of physical, chemical, biological, and other natural systems

This is not just a qualitative picture. This can be made for formal and precise. And gives insights into how networks perform and how to design better optimizers
